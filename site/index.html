Coverage & decision boundary

Physical PTT, microphone, spoken response and phone-independent normal use are the goal. Headphones are preferred, not mandatory. Cellular, secure relay integration and reliable battery operation still require engineering and bench acceptance. Prices and availability are dated observations, not purchase guarantees.

What counts as a complete build?

Board and display; microphone and codec; speech speaker/amplifier or qualified headset path; physical push-to-talk; battery, charging/protection and power supply; cables/connectors; enclosure and fasteners; firmware and a secure agent relay. Cellular additionally requires a compatible modem, antenna, SIM/data plan, power-transient design and compressed-audio transport. A list is not proof that the parts work together.

Purchase only exact cited variants. Confirm bundle contents, stock, shipping and tax at checkout. Assemble and flash licensed firmware; test hold/release, spoken replies, reconnect, power and battery safety before deployment. Existing Wi-Fi, Internet and agent-service access are assumed infrastructure, not included hardware.
